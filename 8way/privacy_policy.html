Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 8 way - Word Puzzle ("the app", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the app. The app is a word puzzle game available for Android and iOS.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The app may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as selected language, completed levels, stars, points, achievements, hints used, and app open progress. This data is used to make the game work and to remember your progress.

Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage, improve gameplay, and fix issues. Firebase Analytics may collect information such as app opens, language selection, level starts, level completions, device type, operating system, approximate region, and other diagnostic or usage events.

Firebase Analytics is provided by Google. You can learn more about how Google handles data at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, and app activity to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

You can learn more about Google advertising privacy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information according to their own policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play services and Apple platform services where applicable

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Storage and Security

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Analytics and advertising data may be processed by Google services. We take reasonable steps to use reputable service providers, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can clear locally stored game progress by deleting the app or clearing the app data from your device settings. You can manage advertising preferences through your device settings, Google settings, Apple settings, or applicable privacy controls available on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
