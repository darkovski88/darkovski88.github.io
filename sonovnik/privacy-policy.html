This Privacy Policy explains how Sonovnik ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use the mobile application. The app uses only Google AdMob (ads) and Google Firebase services.

We do not require account registration and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Some technical and usage data may be collected automatically by third-party services we use (AdMob and Firebase).

2. Advertising and Firebase Services

Sonovnik uses:

These services may automatically collect data such as:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)
  • App interactions, usage events, and ad performance data

Data handling for ads is governed by Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

Any automatically collected data (through AdMob and Firebase) may be used to:

  • Show and optimize ads

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by Google services we use (AdMob and Firebase) to provide advertising, analytics, and diagnostics.

5. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own user account database for this app. Data retention is managed by Google services (AdMob/Firebase) according to their own retention settings and policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app secure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed specifically to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and address the issue.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Limit personalized ads in your device's ad settings
  • Review Google's privacy controls for your account and ads preferences
